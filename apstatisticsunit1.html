Key Takeaways

• Numerical summaries describe the center, spread, and shape of a distribution.
• Mean and median measure center, while standard deviation and IQR measure variability.
• Outliers can greatly affect the mean but have less effect on the median.
• Histograms, boxplots, dotplots, and stemplots visualize distributions.
• Distributions can be symmetric, skewed left, skewed right, or approximately normal.

Must Know Equations

Mean:
x̄ = Σx / n

Range:
Maximum − Minimum

IQR:
Q3 − Q1

Standard Deviation:
s = √[Σ(x−x̄)²/(n−1)]
