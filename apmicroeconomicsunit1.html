Key Takeaways

• Individuals and firms make decisions because resources are scarce.
• Every choice has an opportunity cost.
• Comparative advantage explains why specialization and trade increase efficiency.
• Production Possibilities Curves (PPCs) illustrate scarcity, efficiency, and economic growth.
• Rational decision-makers compare marginal benefits and marginal costs.
