Key Takeaways

• GDP measures the total market value of final goods and services produced within a country.
• Inflation measures the overall increase in prices over time.
• Unemployment measures the percentage of the labor force without jobs.
• The business cycle consists of expansion, peak, contraction, and trough.
• Economic growth improves long-run living standards.

Must Know Equations

GDP = C + I + G + (X − M)

Inflation Rate = [(Current CPI − Previous CPI) / Previous CPI] × 100%

Unemployment Rate = (Unemployed ÷ Labor Force) × 100%
