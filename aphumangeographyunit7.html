Key Takeaways

• Economic development varies across regions due to historical, political, and geographic factors.
• Industrialization changes employment, urbanization, and trade patterns.
• Globalization connects economies through trade and technology.
• Manufacturing location depends on transportation, labor, and market access.
• Economic development can be measured using indicators such as GDP and HDI.
