Key Takeaways

• Firms maximize profit where marginal revenue equals marginal cost.
• Costs include fixed costs, variable costs, and total costs.
• Perfectly competitive firms are price takers.
• Marginal cost eventually rises because of diminishing marginal returns.
• Firms continue operating in the short run if price covers average variable cost.

Must Know Equations

Profit = Total Revenue − Total Cost

Average Total Cost = TC ÷ Q

Average Variable Cost = TVC ÷ Q

Average Fixed Cost = TFC ÷ Q

Marginal Cost = ΔTC ÷ ΔQ
