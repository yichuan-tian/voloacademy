Our Mission

Volo Academy exists to remove barriers to education by organizing the world's best free learning resources into one platform. We aim to help students discover, learn, and grow through carefully selected educational content while inspiring lifelong curiosity and independent learning.

Free Education

Every resource we recommend is freely available so every student can learn without financial barriers.

Quality

We carefully select learning materials from trusted educators, universities, and organizations.

Accessibility

Education should be available anytime, anywhere, for everyone.

Curiosity

Learning doesn't end after school. We encourage exploration throughout life.
