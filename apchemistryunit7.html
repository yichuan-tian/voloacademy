Key Takeaways

• Chemical equilibrium occurs when forward and reverse reaction rates are equal.
• Equilibrium does not mean equal concentrations.
• The equilibrium constant (K) indicates the extent of a reaction.
• Large K favors products; small K favors reactants.
• Le Châtelier's Principle predicts how equilibrium shifts when disturbed.
• Temperature changes alter K; concentration and pressure shifts do not.

Must Know Equations

K = [Products] / [Reactants]

Q = [Products] / [Reactants]
