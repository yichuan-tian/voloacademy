Key Takeaways

• Reaction rate measures how quickly reactants become products.
• Collision Theory explains successful reactions.
• Effective collisions require sufficient energy and correct orientation.
• Catalysts lower activation energy without being consumed.
• Reaction rate depends on concentration, temperature, surface area, and catalysts.
• Rate laws are determined experimentally.
• Integrated rate laws describe concentration changes over time.

Must Know Equations

Rate Law:
Rate = k[A]^m[B]^n

First-order Half-life:
t½ = 0.693 / k
