Key Takeaways

• Thermodynamics studies energy changes during chemical reactions.
• Endothermic reactions absorb heat (ΔH > 0).
• Exothermic reactions release heat (ΔH < 0).
• Potential energy diagrams illustrate energy changes during reactions.
• Hess's Law states that total enthalpy change is independent of reaction pathway.
• Bond enthalpy can be used to estimate reaction enthalpy.
• Entropy (ΔS) measures disorder in a system.
• Gibbs Free Energy determines whether a process is spontaneous.

Must Know Equations

ΔH = Σ(Bonds Broken) − Σ(Bonds Formed)

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS
