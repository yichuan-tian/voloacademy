Key Takeaways

• The French and Indian War increased British debt and colonial tensions.
• British taxation without representation fueled revolutionary sentiment.
• The American Revolution established an independent United States.
• The Articles of Confederation created a weak national government.
• The Constitution strengthened federal authority while protecting individual rights.
