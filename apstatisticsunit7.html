Key Takeaways

• Confidence intervals estimate population means.
• t-distributions are used when the population standard deviation is unknown.
• Larger sample sizes produce more precise estimates.
• Hypothesis tests evaluate claims about population means.
• Statistical conclusions should always be stated in context.

Must Know Equations

Sample Mean:
x̄ = Σx/n

Standard Error:
s/√n

Confidence Interval:
x̄ ± t* × SE

Test Statistic:
t = (x̄−μ₀)/(s/√n)
