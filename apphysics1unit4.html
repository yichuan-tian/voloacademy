Key Takeaways

• Work transfers energy.
• Mechanical energy consists of kinetic and potential energy.
• Total mechanical energy is conserved when non-conservative forces are absent.
• Power measures the rate of doing work.
• Springs store elastic potential energy.

Must Know Equations

Work:
W = Fd cosθ

Kinetic Energy:
KE = ½mv²

Gravitational Potential Energy:
PE = mgh

Elastic Potential Energy:
PE = ½kx²

Power:
P = W/t

Mechanical Energy:
E = KE + PE
