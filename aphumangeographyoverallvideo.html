Key Takeaways

• Human Geography studies how humans interact with places, environments, and spatial patterns.
• Geographic thinking explains why activities occur where they do.
• Population, culture, politics, agriculture, cities, and economic development are interconnected.
• Geographic models help explain spatial organization and predict patterns.
• Globalization influences migration, culture, trade, urbanization, and development.
