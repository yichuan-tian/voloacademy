Key Takeaways

• The derivative represents instantaneous rate of change.
• The derivative is the slope of the tangent line.
• Differentiability requires continuity.
• Basic derivative rules simplify differentiation.
• The Power Rule is one of the most important derivative formulas.

Must Know Equations

Derivative Definition:
f'(x)=lim(h→0)[f(x+h)-f(x)]/h

Power Rule:
d/dx(xⁿ)=nxⁿ⁻¹
