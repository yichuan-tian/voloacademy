Key Takeaways

• Chemical reactions conserve both mass and charge.
• Chemical equations must be balanced.
• Net ionic equations include only reacting species.
• Types of reactions include precipitation, acid-base, oxidation-reduction, and combustion.
• Oxidation involves loss of electrons; reduction involves gain of electrons.
• Stoichiometry uses mole ratios from balanced equations.
• Limiting reactants determine maximum product yield.

Must Know Equations

Percent Yield:
% Yield = (Actual / Theoretical) × 100%
