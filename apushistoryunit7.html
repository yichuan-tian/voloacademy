Key Takeaways

• Progressives sought political, economic, and social reforms.
• The United States became a global power after the Spanish-American War.
• World War I expanded federal power and international involvement.
• The Great Depression challenged American capitalism.
• The New Deal expanded the federal government's role.
• World War II ended the Depression and established the U.S. as a superpower.
