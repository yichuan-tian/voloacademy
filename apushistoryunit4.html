Key Takeaways

• The United States expanded politically, economically, and geographically during the early 19th century.
• Jefferson's presidency strengthened republican ideals while expanding federal power through the Louisiana Purchase.
• The War of 1812 increased American nationalism.
• The Market Revolution transformed transportation, manufacturing, and commerce.
• Jacksonian Democracy expanded political participation for white men while increasing executive power.
