Key Takeaways

• Limits describe the behavior of functions near a point and form the foundation of calculus.
• Derivatives measure instantaneous rates of change and slopes of tangent lines.
• Differentiation is used to analyze functions, solve optimization problems, and model real-world change.
• Integration measures accumulation and area under curves.
• The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus connects derivatives and integrals.
• Differential equations model changing systems over time.
• Calculus emphasizes understanding relationships between functions, rates of change, and accumulation rather than memorizing formulas alone.
