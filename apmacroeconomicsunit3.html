Key Takeaways

• Aggregate Demand (AD) represents total spending in the economy.
• Short-Run Aggregate Supply (SRAS) responds to price changes, while Long-Run Aggregate Supply (LRAS) reflects productive capacity.
• Equilibrium output occurs where AD intersects SRAS.
• Changes in AD and AS explain inflation, recession, and economic growth.
• Fiscal and monetary policies aim to stabilize output and prices.
