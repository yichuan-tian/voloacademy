Key Takeaways

• Ionic bonds form through electron transfer.
• Covalent bonds form through electron sharing.
• Bond length decreases as bond order increases.
• Bond energy increases as bond strength increases.
• Lewis structures show valence electrons and bonding.
• VSEPR Theory predicts molecular geometry.
• Molecular polarity depends on bond polarity and molecular shape.
• Intermolecular forces determine many physical properties.

Must Know Equations
