Key Takeaways

• The Chain Rule differentiates composite functions.
• Implicit differentiation finds derivatives without solving for y.
• Inverse functions require special derivative rules.
• Exponential, logarithmic, and trigonometric functions each have unique derivatives.
• The derivative of an inverse function depends on the derivative of the original function.

Must Know Equations

Chain Rule:
(f(g(x)))' = f'(g(x))·g'(x)

Derivative of eˣ:
d/dx(eˣ)=eˣ

Derivative of ln(x):
d/dx(lnx)=1/x

AP Exam Focus

• Apply the Chain Rule correctly.
• Differentiate implicit equations.
• Differentiate exponential and logarithmic functions.
