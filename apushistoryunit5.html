Key Takeaways

• Westward expansion intensified sectional conflict over slavery.
• Manifest Destiny encouraged territorial expansion.
• Political compromises temporarily delayed the Civil War but failed to resolve sectional tensions.
• The Civil War preserved the Union and ended slavery.
• Reconstruction attempted to rebuild the South and expand civil rights but faced significant resistance.
