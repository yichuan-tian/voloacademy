Key Takeaways

• Markets sometimes fail to allocate resources efficiently.
• Externalities create costs or benefits for third parties.
• Public goods are non-rival and non-excludable.
• Governments use taxes, subsidies, and regulations to correct market failures.
• Property rights encourage efficient resource allocation.
