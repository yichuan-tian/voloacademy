Key Takeaways

• American history is shaped by continuity and change over time.
• Political debates often center on federal versus state power.
• Economic development transformed American society through industrialization and globalization.
• Social reform movements expanded rights and challenged inequality.
• Foreign policy evolved from isolationism to global leadership.
• Historical thinking requires identifying causes, effects, comparisons, and continuity/change over time.
