Key Takeaways

• Matter is composed of atoms containing protons, neutrons, and electrons.
• Atomic number = number of protons; Mass number = protons + neutrons.
• Isotopes have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.
• Average atomic mass is calculated using isotopic abundance.
• Electron configuration follows the Aufbau Principle, Hund's Rule, and Pauli Exclusion Principle.
• Valence electrons determine chemical properties and bonding behavior.
• Coulomb's Law explains attraction between charged particles.
• Photoelectron Spectroscopy (PES) identifies electron energy levels.
