Key Takeaways

• Integration represents accumulation and area under a curve.
• Antiderivatives reverse differentiation.
• Definite integrals calculate net area.
• The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus connects differentiation and integration.
• Riemann Sums approximate definite integrals.

Must Know Equations

Power Rule for Integration:
∫xⁿdx=(xⁿ⁺¹)/(n+1)+C

Fundamental Theorem:
∫ab f(x)dx=F(b)-F(a)
