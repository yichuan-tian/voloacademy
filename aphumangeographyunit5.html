Key Takeaways

• Agriculture has evolved from subsistence farming to commercial production.
• Environmental conditions influence agricultural practices.
• The Agricultural Revolutions transformed food production and population growth.
• Von Thünen's Model explains agricultural land use based on transportation costs.
• Modern agriculture increases productivity but also creates environmental challenges.
