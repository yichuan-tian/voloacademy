Key Takeaways

• Statistical inference estimates population proportions using sample data.
• Confidence intervals estimate plausible values for a population proportion.
• Hypothesis tests evaluate claims about population proportions.
• A p-value measures the strength of evidence against the null hypothesis.
• Statistical significance does not necessarily imply practical significance.

Must Know Equations

Sample Proportion:
p̂ = x/n

Standard Error:
√[p(1−p)/n]

Confidence Interval:
p̂ ± z* × SE

Test Statistic:
z = (p̂−p₀)/SE
