Key Takeaways

• Newton's First Law describes inertia.
• Newton's Second Law relates net force and acceleration.
• Newton's Third Law states forces occur in equal and opposite pairs.
• Free-body diagrams identify forces acting on objects.
• Common forces include gravity, normal force, tension, friction, and spring force.
• Static friction is less than or equal to maximum friction.
