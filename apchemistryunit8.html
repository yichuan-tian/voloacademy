Key Takeaways

• Acids donate H⁺ ions; bases accept H⁺ ions.
• Strong acids and bases completely dissociate.
• Weak acids and bases partially dissociate.
• Water is amphoteric.
• Buffers resist changes in pH.
• Titrations determine unknown concentrations.
• Indicators change color near their pH range.

Must Know Equations

pH = −log[H⁺]

pOH = −log[OH⁻]

pH + pOH = 14

Ka × Kb = Kw = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴
