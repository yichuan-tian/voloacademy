Key Takeaways

• Rotational motion follows principles similar to linear motion.
• Torque causes rotational acceleration.
• Objects rotate about an axis.
• Rotational equilibrium requires zero net torque.
• Center of mass influences balance and stability.
• Angular momentum is conserved when external torque is absent.

Must Know Equations

Torque:
τ = rF sinθ

Rotational Equilibrium:
Στ = 0

Moment of Inertia:
I = Σmr²

Angular Momentum:
L = Iω
