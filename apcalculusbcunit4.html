Key Takeaways

• Derivatives describe rates of change in real-world situations.
• Related Rates problems connect multiple changing variables.
• Linear approximation estimates function values near known points.
• Mean Value Theorem relates average and instantaneous rates of change.
• Optimization finds maximum or minimum values under constraints.

Must Know Equations

Point-Slope Form:
y−y₁=m(x−x₁)

Linear Approximation:
L(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x−a)
