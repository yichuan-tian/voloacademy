Key Takeaways

• Scatterplots reveal relationships between two quantitative variables.
• Correlation measures the direction and strength of a linear relationship.
• Correlation does not imply causation.
• Least-squares regression predicts one variable from another.
• Residuals measure prediction error.

Must Know Equations

Least-Squares Regression Line:
ŷ = a + bx

Residual:
Observed − Predicted

Coefficient of Determination:
r²
