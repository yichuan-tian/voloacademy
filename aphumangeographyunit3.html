Key Takeaways

• Culture consists of shared beliefs, practices, languages, and traditions.
• Languages spread through migration, trade, and conquest.
• Religions influence cultural landscapes and settlement patterns.
• Cultural diffusion spreads ideas between societies.
• Globalization promotes both cultural convergence and cultural diversity.
